# Development acceptance contract

The lane passes V1 only when all of the following hold:

1. Every registered source exists at its frozen SHA-256 and byte count.
2. The audited engine commit and both Python/configuration fingerprints are
   recorded together; a Python-only match is insufficient.
3. The structural atlas starts from 227 source-level representatives, removes
   two canonical full-action duplicates, and retains 225 unique tapes. It
   reproduces 102,803 nodes, depth 719, and the deduplicated turn-78 two-child
   boundary with aggregate support 97. Legacy duplicated support and entropy
   are never used; only the exact legacy prefix-set digest is retained as a
   structure cross-check. The manifest and every retained feature/tape are covered by
   one frozen 451-file content inventory digest.
4. Canonical IDs round-trip, opponent identity/private opponent selectors and
   cross-lane parents are rejected, and both incumbent lanes remain separate.
   V1 schema version, own-private paths, integer types, motif/patch fields, and
   one-patch-per-step runtime attribution are closed and validated.
5. WDL evaluation is explicitly single-lane, matched-control, both-seat, fresh,
   technically valid, and net-positive before it can be efficacy-eligible.
   Exact engine/candidate/control hashes, unique predeclared contexts,
   reward-derived outcomes, strict booleans, and the consumed-seed registry are
   validated rather than accepted as labels. The complete protocol/context
   manifest must be registry-identical, and the immutable lane-local candidate
   artifact is re-hashed and byte-checked from its registered workspace path.
   V1 additionally keeps `formal_efficacy_authorized=false`; a passing raw WDL
   gate is reported but cannot become efficacy-eligible in this lane.
6. Missing raw c27 and fixed-horizon-25 evidence is labeled `summary_only`; the
   frozen Boatlee E2 selector with actual win delta zero is not efficacy.
   Aggregate fixture classification is permanently proposal-only regardless of
   a caller-supplied freshness label.
7. Branches are bounded to 1–48 turns, use explicit rejoin compatibility, and
   fail closed to the incumbent on missing/malformed state or horizon failure.
   Observation step/player and state counts/coordinates require exact,
   non-Boolean integers; coercible strings and Booleans cannot trigger a branch.
8. Generated candidates are under 5 MiB, standard-library-only, isolated
   importable, expose both entrypoints, cap market orders at ten, and project
   atomic same-crop PLANT demand before returning. Compilation and technical
   evaluation both enforce the size and aggregate pass decision.
   The compiler re-hashes its frozen incumbent and publishes atomically only
   after static audit, isolated import, and both entrypoints pass. Resolved
   outputs beneath incumbent and E1/E2 source/evidence roots are rejected.
9. Existing Async V3 (23 tests) and main-approach source contracts (3 tests)
   remain green. Frozen old full-tape tests are out of scope because their
   archived input and import-time Kaggle dependency are absent.

Passing means `READY_FOR_PROPOSAL_ONLY`. It selects no fresh seeds, executes no
formal efficacy games, and grants no promotion or submission authority.
