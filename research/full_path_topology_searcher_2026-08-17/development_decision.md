# Development decision — 2026-08-17

Decision: **REPRODUCIBLE_BRANCH_EXPERIMENTATION_SUBSTRATE_ONLY**.

The isolated V1 implements frozen evidence registration, exact engine and
configuration re-fingerprinting, a read-only 225-unique-tape action atlas with
full/unit/market entropy, causal divergence forensics, evidence-derived proposal
diagnostics, canonical branch/state/outcome contracts, bounded standalone
compilation, natural rejoin/fallback, and WDL-primary evaluation.

This is explicitly a partial implementation of `topology_path.md`: each
incumbent graph is a 719-node linear spine and the compiler materializes one
public trigger plus bounded patches and continuation/rejoin. It does not yet
represent multiple calibrated outgoing branches from a state node or route by
conditional WDL value, so it is not `BEST_POLICY_TOPOLOGY`.

Development verification starts with 227 source-level representatives, removes
two exact full-action duplicates, and reproduces 102,803 trie nodes, depth 719,
and the deduplicated turn-78 two-child boundary with aggregate support 97. Its
complete 451-file retained manifest/feature/tape input inventory is
content-fingerprinted; legacy duplicated support statistics are not used. The generated
technical candidate is isolated-importable, standard-library-only, and exposes
both required entrypoints. Exact counts and digests are recorded in the tracked
`development_verification_summary.json`.

Calibration does not claim to distinguish all three anchors. The report-only
c27 13–7 positive control and fixed-horizon-25 distribution control remain
blocked because their raw paired rows are unavailable. It does reject the frozen Boatlee E2 selector as efficacy:
its contexts are consumed and actual win delta is zero despite favorable
paired-margin and own-bank diagnostics.

Proposal ranking is also fail-closed. Atlas mutability and consumed own-loss
matches are derived from registered evidence, but no audited per-motif,
multi-lineage frontier-code match artifact exists yet. Consequently there are
zero ranked proposals; the former hand-entered scalar priorities were removed.

No incumbent, E1/E2 source, generated incumbent artifact, consumed seed, fresh
seed, formal game, remote repository, or Kaggle state was changed. The next
permitted action is to author and technically evaluate lane-local bounded
proposals. Fresh paired efficacy remains separately authorized work.

WDL evaluation is closed to registry-predeclared protocol/context manifests and
re-hashes the immutable lane-local candidate artifact before admitting evidence.
No fresh paired protocol is registered in V1, so caller-invented candidate hashes
cannot reach the mechanical WDL gate.

Compact machine-readable evidence is tracked at
`research/full_path_topology_searcher_2026-08-17/development_verification_summary.json`.
