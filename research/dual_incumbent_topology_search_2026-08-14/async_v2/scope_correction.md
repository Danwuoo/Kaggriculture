research question.  It contained only eight mutation-free ninth-cow roots per
lane: removal and shifts `-72,-48,-24,0,+24,+48,+72`.  It did not evolve

lane as negative/provenance evidence.  They do not promote a candidate and do
not unlock S2.  Fresh seeds `20260814`, `20260815`, and `20260816` are consumed
and must never be relabeled as v2 holdouts.

preflight and explicit authorization after development verification; no v2
